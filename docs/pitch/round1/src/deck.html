Satyapan
सत्यापन · “verification” in Hindi
Payment verification that a deepfake cannot pass.
One-sentence solution
Satyapan makes a deepfaked CFO or vendor unable to move money: every payment is checked against the company’s own vendor master, the real executive’s voice and approvals carry a cryptographic Satyapan Seal, and any doubt hands verification to a person and channel the attacker does not control.
“A deepfake can copy a CFO’s voice, never his key.”
Awaaz nakli ho sakti hai, mohar nahi. (A voice can be faked; the seal cannot.)

The deepfake “boss scam” has reached India’s finance desks

It is happening in India, now
JULY 2026 · ₹10.70 CRORE · LISTED-COMPANY SUBSIDIARY [R1]
Starmangalsutra Pvt Ltd, a subsidiary of Sky Gold & Diamonds, disclosed that an employee sent funds to unknown accounts on instructions from an impersonated director. The company cited deepfake techniques, including profile cloning, and a compromised company phone and laptop. The director had given no such instruction.
17 JULY 2026 · SEBI CAUTION TO LISTED COMPANIES, PR 40/2026 [R2]
“Boss scam”: CEO/MD impersonation using AI deepfakes and voice cloning on video calls, hijacked WhatsApp Web sessions, the attacker’s number saved under the CEO’s name, and “don’t tell anyone, it is price-sensitive”. Then a mule account. SEBI’s advice: verify directly with the senior official, never pay on a message alone, report on 1930.
2024 · ARUP, HONG KONG · HK$200M (ABOUT US$25.6M) [R3]
An employee made 15 transfers after a video call with a deepfaked CFO and colleagues. The person who should have verified was the person being deceived.
Scale: Indian citizens lost ₹22,845.73 crore to cyber fraud in 2024 (Lok Sabha reply, 22 Jul 2025; mostly consumer fraud) [R4]. Corporate losses come on top, and most are never disclosed.
Primary user
Priya, accounts-payable maker at a ₹80-crore Pune manufacturer (illustrative). Pays vendors by NEFT / RTGS / UPI. Checker: Arjun. CFO: Rajesh.
Harmful moment
Priya is about to pay a new or changed bank account, in a hurry and in secret, because “the CFO” or “the vendor” said so.
Exact threat pattern
Harm
Loss of lakhs to crores in minutes. Recovery depends on reporting the transaction reference (UTR) within hours. Listed firms must disclose; Sky Gold’s shares fell on the news [R1].
Sharply bounded problem
In scope: payment instructions reaching an SME or mid-market AP team by email, WhatsApp text or voice note, and browser meetings (Google Meet, Teams on the web).
Out of scope (MVP): consumer scams, phone-call interception, native Teams / Zoom desktop apps (upload the recording instead).

A trusted floor, raise-only evidence, and humans the attacker has not reached

Safety rules, property-tested on the real engine (7 test files)
  • Untrusted content can only raise the band, never lower it.
  • The floor reads only trusted data (fuzzed with hostile inputs).
  • LLM or style signals: at most VERIFY, never counted as strong.
  • No HOLD from a single uncorroborated ask (policy 1.1).
  • Only the checker can clear a HOLD; the maker never can.
  • No code path files a report; a human always submits.
Detectors decide who verifies, never whether money to a new or changed account needs verifying.
A deepfake works by manipulating the person who would verify. Any strong signal therefore moves the decision to someone else.
