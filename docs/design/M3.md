# M3 Design Note — Multimedia Tampering & Manipulation Detection

## 1. Objective
Build `src/satyapan/forensics/tamper.py` to detect post-capture manipulation across images, video, and audio, producing visual heatmaps/spectrograms and non-absolutist forensic findings (`consistent with` / `inconsistent with`). Every check operates exclusively on a verified working copy.

## 2. Algorithms & Visual Artifacts
### 2.1 Image Tampering (`analyze_image_tampering`)
1. **Error Level Analysis (`ela_analysis`)**:
   - Re-encodes at JPEG $Q=90$, computes pixel absolute difference normalized by local Sobel edge gradient (so sharp natural edges do not trigger false alarms), and identifies $16\times 16$ block outliers via robust MAD Z-scores. Outputs `ela_heatmap.png`.
2. **Double-JPEG & Quantization-Table Analysis (`double_jpeg_analysis`)**:
   - Inspects JPEG quantization tables (standard IJG vs custom/re-saved tables) and computes $8\times 8$ DCT AC coefficient histogram periodicity (comb effect from $Q_1 \to Q_2$ double quantization) plus $8\times 8$ block-grid spatial inconsistency (where a spliced patch breaks the background JPEG grid).
3. **Copy-Move Forgery Detection (`copy_move_analysis`)**:
   - Extracts DCT/gradient descriptors on overlapping $16\times 16$ non-uniform blocks, lexicographically matches near-identical blocks separated by $\ge 24\text{ px}$, and clusters displacement vectors $(\Delta x, \Delta y)$ (plus ORB keypoint matching when textured keypoints exist) to locate cloned regions. Outputs `copy_move_heatmap.png`.
4. **Noise-Residual Inconsistency (`noise_residual_analysis`)**:
   - Computes edge-suppressed Laplacian high-pass noise residuals across $16\times 16$ blocks and detects regional noise variance anomalies (spliced foreign sensor noise or zero-noise airbrushed/inpainted regions). Outputs `noise_residual_heatmap.png` and 4-panel `tamper_composite.png`.

### 2.2 Video Tampering (`analyze_video_tampering`)
- **Duplicated / Dropped Frames**: Computes inter-frame pixel difference $D_t = \text{mean}(|I_t - I_{t-1}|)$ and PTS deltas $\Delta t_i$. Flags frozen/duplicated runs ($D_t \approx 0$ during active motion) and frame-drop / splice spikes ($Z_{\text{MAD}}(D_t) > 5.0$).
- **Re-encoding Signs**: Measures $8\times 8$ macroblock boundary energy ratio and stream GOP/quantization shifts. Outputs `video_tamper_timeline.png`.

### 2.3 Audio Tampering (`analyze_audio_tampering`)
- **Splice & Discontinuity Detection**: Computes short-time spectral flux spikes, waveform first-difference transients, and inter-segment noise-floor dB shifts (detecting cut-and-paste splices between different acoustic environments). Outputs `audio_splice_spectrogram.png`.

## 3. Evaluation Protocol
- Measured on a seeded synthetic tampering benchmark (`research/forensics/eval_m3_tamper.py`, `SEED = 20261103`) covering authentic controls vs (a) spliced/inpainted images, (b) copy-move forged images, (c) double-JPEG compressed images, (d) frame-dropped and frame-duplicated videos, and (e) spliced audio recordings.
