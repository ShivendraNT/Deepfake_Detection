# M2 Design Note — Deepfake & Synthetic Media Detection Engine

## 1. Objective
Build `src/satyapan/forensics/deepfake.py`, unifying Satyapan's face-swap (`GenD` CLIP ViT-L/14 ONNX) and voice-clone (`Gustking` XLS-R ONNX) detectors with a new whole-image AI-generated (diffusion/GAN) detector and frame-level + aggregate video analysis. All findings follow non-absolutist forensic language (`consistent with` / `inconsistent with`) with calibrated confidence and stated limits.

## 2. Architecture & Modalities
1. **Image Deepfake & Synthetic Engine (`analyze_image_deepfake`)**:
   - **Face-Swap Check**: Reuses `satyapan.imagecheck.face.ImageFaceChecker` (`gend_clip_l14.onnx`, $\tau_{\text{hi}} = 4.6858$, $\tau_{\text{lo}} = 3.7513$ at 1% / 5% val FPR). Abstains honestly when no face $\ge 64\text{ px}$ is present.
   - **Whole-Image AI-Generated (Diffusion/GAN) Detector**: Extracts 6 complementary forensic features on the full image:
     1. `clip_whole_logit`: Whole-image center + quadrant crop logit from `gend_clip_l14.onnx` (CLIP ViT-L/14);
     2. `fft_hf_ratio`: 2D FFT azimuthal radial power ratio in high spatial frequencies ($f \in [0.28, 0.48]$ Nyquist);
     3. `checkerboard_index`: Periodic spectral peak energy at Nyquist/2 and Nyquist/4 from transposed-convolution/upsampling grids;
     4. `photon_transfer_violation`: Deviation from physical camera shot-noise luminance proportionality ($\sigma^2(I) \propto I$);
     5. `cfa_cross_channel_corr`: High-pass residual cross-color correlation ($R-G$, $G-B$) characteristic of optical Bayer CFA demosaicing;
     6. `patch_noise_uniformity`: Coefficient of variation of local $16\times 16$ noise-residual variances.
   - Combined via a logistic head (`config/whole_image_synthetic.json`) calibrated on a validation split so $\text{FPR} \le 5\%$ at $\tau_{\text{hi}}$.
2. **Video Deepfake Engine (`analyze_video_deepfake`)**:
   - Samples frames across the video, computing **frame-level scores** (face + whole-frame synthetic score per timestamp) and **6-second window fusion scores** (`satyapan.video.analyzer.VideoAnalyzer` with GenD, rPPG, lighting, and lip-sync).
   - Aggregates frame-level (`p90`, `max`, `mean`, `flagged_ratio`) and window-level verdicts (`classify_windows`).
3. **Audio Voice-Clone Engine (`analyze_audio_deepfake`)**:
   - Splits 16 kHz mono audio into 5-second windows, scores each window with `satyapan.voice.detector.OnnxVoiceDetector` (`gustking_xlsr`), and applies the two-consecutive-window rule (`satyapan.voice.rules.classify_windows`) with speech-duration gating ($\ge 3\text{ s}$).

## 3. Calibration & Evaluation Protocol
- Validation split sets $\tau_{\text{hi}}$ (target $\le 2\%$ FPR) and $\tau_{\text{lo}}$ (target $\le 5\%$ FPR).
- Held-out test split is scored once for AUC, TPR, and FPR with 95% confidence intervals, plus a JPEG Q60 re-encoding compression-robustness test (`research/forensics/eval_m2_deepfake.py`).
