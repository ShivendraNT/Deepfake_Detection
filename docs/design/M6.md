# M6 Design Note — Automated Multimedia Forensic Report & Section 63 BSA 2023 Certificate

## 1. Objective
Build `src/satyapan/forensics/section63.py` (reused from Pramaan with attribution) and `src/satyapan/forensics/report.py` to generate a reproducible, court-aware **Multimedia Forensic Examination Report** in PDF, HTML, Markdown, and JSON formats directly from stored case data.

## 2. Report Contents
Every generated report (`forensic_report.{pdf,html,md,json}` under `<case_dir>/report/`) includes:
1. **Case & Integrity Header**: Case ID, Title, Agency, FIR Reference, Examiner, Locker Verification Status (`PASSED` / `FAILED`), Custody Chain Head, and RFC 6962 Merkle Root.
2. **Schedule of Sealed Multimedia Evidence**: Evidence ID, filename, modality, byte size, intake SHA-256 hash, read-only (`0o444`) verification, and Merkle inclusion status.
3. **Signed Chain of Custody Log**: Full sequence of `intake`, `working_copy`, and `analyze` entries with timestamps, actors, SHA-256 chain links, and Ed25519 signature status.
4. **Forensic Methodology**: Summary of M1–M4 methods, calibration protocols, and decision boundaries.
5. **Per-Item Findings & Embedded Visual Evidence**: Non-absolutist finding (`consistent with` / `inconsistent with`), confidence score, technical `"Why"` indicators, subcheck metrics, and embedded heatmap/spectrogram PNGs (`tamper_composite.png`, `ela_heatmap.png`, `deepfake_spectrum.png`, `audio_splice_spectrogram.png`, `video_tamper_timeline.png`, `thumbnail_comparison.png`).
6. **Methodological Limitations**: Explicit caveats per modality and global forensic boundaries.
7. **Draft Section 63 (Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023) Certificate (Parts A & B)**: Reused from Pramaan (`pramaan/report/section63.py`), covering Part A (Person-in-Charge declaration & Device Schedule) and Part B (Section 63(4)(c) Technical Expert Certificate with SHA-256 FIPS 180-4, Ed25519 RFC 8032, and RFC 6962 Merkle proofs), clearly watermarked `DRAFT`.
8. **Examiner Sign-Off & Peer Review Block**.

## 3. Reproducibility & Offline PDF Architecture
- By defaulting `generated_at_utc` to the stored `analysis.json` timestamp (or an explicit `fixed_timestamp`), re-running `generate_forensic_report(locker)` on stored case data produces **byte-identical** Markdown, JSON, HTML, and PDF files.
- The PDF engine writes native PDF 1.4 vector text streams (`Helvetica`, `Helvetica-Bold`, `Courier`) so `pdfplumber` and any PDF viewer can search/extract every hash and finding, and embeds forensic heatmaps/spectrograms as `XObject /Image /DCTDecode` streams on the finding pages.
