# M7 Design Note — Comprehensive Tests, Golden-File Verification & Adversarial Red-Team

## 1. Objective
Verify every forensic module (`locker`, `deepfake`, `tamper`, `provenance`, `pipeline`, `section63`, `report`, `routes_forensics`) through unit tests, golden-file report tests, an end-to-end test on a committed multi-modal demo kit (`demo_kit/`), and a 5-attack adversarial red-team benchmark (`research/forensics/eval_m7_adversarial.py`).

## 2. Committed Demo Kit (`demo_kit/`)
Generated deterministically by `scripts/make_forensic_demo_kit.py`:
- `01_authentic_c2pa_photo.jpg`: Camera-style JPEG with Sony EXIF and valid Ed25519 C2PA JUMBF manifest;
- `02_synthetic_gan_scene.png`: AI-generated image with spectral checkerboard grid and ComfyUI metadata;
- `03_tampered_copymove_splice.jpg`: Copy-move + spliced patch + Photoshop EXIF + inverted timestamp + mismatched thumbnail;
- `04_whatsapp_reencoded_photo.jpg`: Stripped-EXIF `4:2:0` Q68 social-messaging re-encode;
- `05_spliced_audio_recording.wav`: 16 kHz audio with room noise-floor shift and sample splice transient;
- `06_manipulated_video_clip.mp4`: Video clip with duplicated freeze-frames and a dropped-frame splice jump;
- `manifest.json` & `golden_section63_part_a.md`: Pinned SHA-256 hashes, expected findings, and golden Section 63 Part A output.

## 3. Five-Attack Adversarial Red-Team (`research/forensics/eval_m7_adversarial.py`)
Evaluates detector TPR, FPR, and AUC before and after 5 attacker countermeasures (`SEED = 20261107`, $n = 30$ tampered/synthetic + $30$ authentic per attack):
1. **JPEG Q50 Re-encode**: Heavy lossy re-compression;
2. **Downscale & Upscale Resize (`0.5x -> 1.0x`)**: Destroys pixel-grid alignment and high-frequency Nyquist peaks;
3. **15% Border Crop**: Shifts $8\times 8$ JPEG block grids and removes peripheral regions;
4. **Additive Gaussian Noise ($\sigma = 6.0$)**: Masks fine ELA and sensor-noise differences;
5. **Complete Metadata & C2PA Stripping**: Strips all EXIF, XMP, PNG text chunks, and C2PA JUMBF manifests to measure pixel-only fallback detection.
