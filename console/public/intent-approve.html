What you are signing

Your passkey signs a hash of: case, requester, amount in rupees, a hash of the full account number, IFSC, a one-time nonce and an expiry.

If anyone changes the amount or the account after you approve, the signature no longer matches and the payment is refused. The same approval cannot be replayed.

Approving here does not clear a HOLD on its own: the checker's callback rules still apply.
