      What Satya Pocket is — and isn't.
      
            
      A small Satya for your phone, with the same engine and the same rule: evidence can
              only raise an alarm, never clear one.
      
          
      
          
      
            
      What it does
      
            
      Checks a message, screenshot, voice note, photo,
              PDF or video through the Satya engine on the laptop. Explains why in plain words.
              Drafts your 1930 / bank / NCRP report after money has left. Mirrors a live call alert
              and sends OOB-Confirm to the real approver.
      
            
      What it does not do
      
            
      It does not listen to phone calls.
              iOS and Android do not let an app capture call audio. The laptop companion listens to
              the other side of a call only after you say yes; the phone never opens the microphone.
              

      It does not set a band. The band comes only from the
              Satya engine on the laptop. This app displays it; it cannot compute or change one.
              

      It does not file anything. Every draft is MANUAL_ONLY.
              A human calls 1930 and presses Submit.

      Put it on your home screen
      
            
      iPhone: Safari → Share →
              Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome → menu → Add to Home screen.
              It opens without browser chrome. The app shell still loads from the laptop, so keep
              the laptop awake and on the same hotspot.
      
            
      On the laptop, open Notebook → Now → On your phone to show the QR codes.
              Phone and laptop must be on the same Wi-Fi/hotspot. Nothing on this page is stored.
